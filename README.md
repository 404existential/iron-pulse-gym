# ⚡ R/NESS • Redefine Your Limits

A luxury minimalist website for **R/NESS**, a premier high-performance fitness studio based in Indiranagar, Bengaluru. Inspired by high-end editorial aesthetics and luxury spaces.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **🦾 Scattered 3D Floating Dumbbells & Golden Spheres:**
  - Depth-of-field physics canvas scattered across the entire hero.
  - Realistic drop shadows, specular metallic highlights, and varying perspective depths (inspired by the floating coffee beans & golden pearls aesthetic).
  - Cursor repulsion physics: elements glide smoothly away when pushed by the cursor and move out of the screen, re-entering with natural damping.
  - Click shockwave pulse.

- **🏛️ Clean, Concise Architectural Layout:**
  - Elegant typography pairing: **Playfair Display** (luxury editorial serif) + **DM Sans** (clean modern sans).
  - Pill badges and smooth rounded action buttons (`border-radius: 50px`).
  - The R/NESS Difference: Compact, well-proportioned highlight cards.
  - Master Coaches showcase with photos.
  - Transparent pricing in Indian Rupees (₹).
  - Indiranagar studio location and direct WhatsApp concierge integration.
